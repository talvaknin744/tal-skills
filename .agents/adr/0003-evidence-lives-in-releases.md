# Evaluation evidence lives in releases

Evaluation results must identify an immutable candidate and remain associated with the released evidence. Preserve historical prompts, fixtures, verifiers, rubrics and results; when a contract changes, add a versioned case and explain the reason instead of rewriting history.

## Consequences

Reports distinguish executed, unexecuted and blocked coverage, and name both candidate and environment identities. Exact-digest exceptions remain narrow and temporary until the planned Phase 5 consolidation.
