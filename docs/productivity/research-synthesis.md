The split follows independent invocation needs. Planning is useful before a session, tutoring during one, and experimentation when a recurring bottleneck appears. Each skill is self-contained. Its ordinary steps stay in the entrypoint; mathematics, scheduling, state transitions and source details are disclosed through conditional references. No study request requires loading the entire research catalog.

## Consolidated decisions

**Start with the performance, then select the method.** Recall of a definition, choosing a theorem, executing it, writing a proof and performing under time pressure are separate demands. A useful output in everyday life can instead be a decision or working artifact. Align the practice and assess the corresponding performance. Source examples include [professional learning](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l7h1D2uA09I&t=113s) and [strategy versus execution](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=scPiO65csRI&t=680s).

**Retain retrieval alongside meaningful organization.** Strong encoding rhetoric elsewhere in the corpus is qualified by explicit support for spaced retrieval and recognition-versus-generation checks. The coach asks for an actual answer before feedback, tests new cues and treats correction as preparation for a later independent attempt. [Recognition warning](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNu-pEHvZd4&t=3953s), [beginner priorities](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nfoCpMuH9Tc&t=725s).

**Keep maps provisional and mathematics exact.** Comparison and grouping can reveal useful structure, but a diagram must distinguish implication, equivalence, prerequisite and analogy. Retain domains, hypotheses, quantifiers and complete proofs. Source notes, scratch work and closed-book reconstruction serve different purposes. AI can help with documentation or a needed explanation while later independent work supplies learning evidence. [Documentation exception](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rHzmiH7bSNA&t=164s), [analogy tradeoffs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jdMJcrqZuC8&t=1399s).

**Make uncertainty actionable.** Identify the first consequential error or the next unanswered question. A missing condition is an observation; its cause remains a hypothesis until checked. Supply instruction when prerequisites prevent progress. Leave a precise restart question with a source location after an interruption, and explicitly revisit material deferred during layered reading. [Restart question](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6RlmL6If1bU&t=627s), [overload qualification](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mQtSv7ZbbIw&t=3255s).

**Measure the whole cost of reaching usable knowledge.** Count preparation, study, review, correction and verification. Record assistance, familiarity, correctness and time separately. Plan from actual available capacity, with breaks and repair inside it. A missed appointment remains missed; a new plan does not silently create another original time budget. [Whole-route comparison](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=goHaIfMcfWo&t=3288s).

**Test one process change at a time.** Trace an actual topic through exposure, practice and assessment. Choose a small replacement, state its time cost and test it on a comparable fresh task. Use confidence before feedback to locate miscalibration, not to assign mastery. Useful experiments can remain inconclusive. [Process mapping](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QeOfsMHc7E4&t=414s), [confidence calibration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8t-vKmzyW5E&t=1112s).

## Tensions resolved rather than copied

| Claim or tension in the corpus | Rule retained |
|---|---|
| Strong understanding is described as preventing forgetting, yet other videos explicitly recommend retrieval | Keep delayed tests and varied application; no permanent-memory promise |
| More effort and confusion are praised, while live coaching reduces overload | Select difficulty from performance and prerequisite knowledge; support a blocked learner |
| Linear notes and AI explanations are criticized, yet documentation and examples are also permitted | Match the aid to its purpose; verify source precision and test independent use |
| Extreme study hours and motivational pressure coexist with recovery advice | Use actual capacity, preserve recovery and treat fallbacks as partial work |
| Short sessions are praised, while exams require extended reasoning | Use short recall where it fits; preserve long problems and simulations |
| Coaching ladders and quizzes assign levels and percentiles | Diagnose concrete task failures instead of fixed learner identities |
| Fast reading and polished maps look efficient | Evaluate total time, delayed correctness and transfer |
| Numerical claims promise dramatic gains | Exclude unverified percentages, universal thresholds and neurological explanations from operational rules |

## Independent evidence and its limits

[Karpicke and Blunt (2011)](https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2011/2011_Karpicke_Blunt_Science.pdf) found benefits of retrieval on later comprehension and inference for studied science texts relative to the tested alternatives. This supports including retrieval, not a universal ranking of all note formats or a guarantee for a calculus exam. [Cepeda et al. (2008)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19076480/) shows that useful spacing changes with the retention horizon; it does not establish the skills' adjustable +1/+3/+7-day starter sequence as an optimum. These primary sources were reopened during this update. Other research discussions in the older local report retain their own stated verification limits.

The skills' scoring labels, state transitions, time allocations and protected-mock rules are engineering and teaching design choices. The combined package has not been tested for learning gains. Sponsor mentions, program surveys, personal trials of AI products and neuroscience analogies are not independent efficacy evidence. Historical product observations are not current product recommendations.
