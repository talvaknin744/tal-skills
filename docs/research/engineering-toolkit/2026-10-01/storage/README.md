The crawl inventory is preserved in the [research crawl archive manifest](../../../MANIFEST.md#archived-files) and its external archive. It stores URL, title, date, metadata provenance, prior-ledger matches, and explicit read status. **1,451 titles are inferred slug labels**, principally MongoDB's sitemap-only records; they are labelled and are not exact article titles. Publication dates are nullable; sitemap lastmod remains a separate field. [coverage.json](../../../MANIFEST.md#file-docs-research-engineering-toolkit-2026-10-01-storage-coverage-json) records the exact collection, endpoints/pages/counts, terminal evidence, earliest observed dates, gaps, and exclusions. Terminal evidence establishes the accessible endpoint boundary; it cannot establish coverage of deleted, private, or unlinked publisher history.

[request-log.json](../../../MANIFEST.md#file-docs-research-engineering-toolkit-2026-10-01-storage-request-log-json) retains 508 request evidence records: URL, status, UTC retrieval time, response-byte SHA-256, size, and available pagination headers. Acquisitions used bounded 8-second connect/25-second read timeouts and at least 1.05 seconds between initiated fetches per host. The sole observed redirect changed hosts; automatic redirect hops were not independently logged. [collector.py](collector.py) replays declared collection endpoints, paces and records each redirect hop, and extracts metadata without retaining bodies. A future replay must separately check new totals, continuation links, and repeated page identities before claiming terminal coverage.

All raw response bodies, feed article content, CMS HTML, downloaded HTML/RSC, script excerpts, and duplicate scratch inventories were removed before freeze. The canonical index, coverage, evidence log, original findings, and useful replay collector remain. No full copyrighted article prose is published.
