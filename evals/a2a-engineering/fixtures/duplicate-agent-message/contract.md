A2A specification 1.0.1, wire 1.0. SendMessage deduplication is optional. Alice sends message m4 commanding business operation transfer-8; a lost response causes a repeated SendMessage. The peer may create a task for each accepted message. Bob learns Alice's task ID and calls get and cancel. Cancellation is idempotent as a protocol operation, but a completion race may mean cancellation does not succeed. The application promises one transfer for accepted transfer-8, scoped to Alice and its original payload.
