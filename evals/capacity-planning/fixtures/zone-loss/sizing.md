# Service capacity proposal
There are three availability zones with four ready replicas per zone. A representative single-replica test sustained 180 correct, on-time requests/s within the 200 ms latency objective. The proposed peak is 1500 requests/s, evenly distributed with the same mix. The database workload test sustained 1400 requests/s for this application's query mix within its objective; beyond that, pool waits grew. No higher database limit has been demonstrated.
The proposal multiplies 12 replicas by 180 and declares sufficient peak and zone-failure capacity. On one-zone loss only eight replicas remain. Replacement replicas need 90 seconds to start and warm their caches; the cold-cache database demand has not been measured. Retries can increase downstream attempts, but their effective ratio is unrecorded.
The three-zone layout and database results are inputs to review, not guarantees of linear fleet scaling or independent failures. Infrastructure costs per ready replica are known, but no cost-per-useful-request comparison has been made.
