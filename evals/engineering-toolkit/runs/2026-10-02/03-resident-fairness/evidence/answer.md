**Smallest feasible contract.** Keep one *actually free* slot at time 1, hold at most three A jobs resident, and leave A4 at the broker. Dispatch B1 then C1 immediately. Once both are complete, this closed trace permits A4 to start at 3. One slot is the mathematical minimum because it supplies exactly the two slot-seconds B/C need between times 1 and 3. It has **zero time for delivery, dispatch, release, or cleanup delay**. If future B/C arrivals must also be protected, keep that slot available while the promise applies; A4 then starts at 8 and still finishes before its deadline of 20.

For a policy with timing margin, keep two slots available for B/C and run only A1/A2 initially. Both B/C jobs can start as late as time 2 and finish by 3. A borrowed slot counts toward that reserve only after its local state has actually been released; a cancellation response is not reclaim. Thus the draft’s running A3/A4 cannot borrow it. Prefetched A could borrow only with a demonstrated release and dispatch bound that fits the remaining time.

**Verification plan, unexecuted:** Model each arrival, receive, start, cancellation, actual release, finish, and acknowledgement. At every event, the capacity oracle is `resident_A + resident_B + resident_C <= 4`, counting prefetch, waits, execution, and cleanup. For the one-slot schedule, assert a free slot at time 1, B1 finishes at 2, C1 finishes at 3, and every A job finishes by 20. For the two-slot schedule, assert two usable slots by time 2 and both B/C finishes by 3. Repeat with B/C arriving immediately after A borrowing begins and with positive reclaim or dispatch delay; any missed bound fails the policy. Track returned A jobs by stable broker identity and count A progress only after successful settlement and acknowledgement. No worker or broker experiment has established these timing bounds.

**Utilization:** Compare the same horizon and distinguish execution from residency. Every fully executed schedule uses `4×8 + 2×1 = 34` slot-seconds. Comparing FIFO at 9 with a reserve at 16 cannot establish superior utilization. More consequentially, FIFO spends two slot-seconds on **late** B/C work, while the feasible schedules complete all six jobs by their deadlines.
