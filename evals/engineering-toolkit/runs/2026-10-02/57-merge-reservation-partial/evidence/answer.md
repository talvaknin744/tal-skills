**The proposed acceptance path fails the contract.** Each region can atomically accept one distinct reservation while disconnected, because each sees its own one-unit balance. The two final receipts total **2 accepted units against 1 available unit**, violating the rule in [contract.md](../final-project/contract.md). Local atomicity, unique IDs, and retry deduplication do not coordinate those two decisions. The claim in [proposal.md](../final-project/proposal.md) that eventual agreement implies valid acceptance is therefore false.

The replica observations establish **convergence, not correctness**: duplicate deliveries add no extra records, both sets end with A and B, and remaining inventory is −1. The independently declared `accepted` outcomes are the receipts in [observations.json](../final-project/observations.json); the file is an authored scenario, not a runtime trace. Its 12-second, A-only display is within the contract’s permitted 60-second incomplete-view window and was not used to authorize a reservation. A primary display read or five-second wait cannot make a later local acceptance safe.

**Smallest supported repair:** enforce the one-unit limit at final acceptance with a shared, durable, atomic conditional reservation per SKU, including operation-ID deduplication in that decision. Return `accepted` only after that decision commits; when the authority is unreachable, return a nonfinal unavailable or pending result. If final acceptance must continue during a partition, preallocate enforceable rights: with one unit, only one region can hold the right. The already declared pair of final receipts cannot be made valid retroactively under the supplied contract.

Check display freshness separately against its 60-second allowance. The arithmetic run is insufficient evidence to ship the acceptance path.
