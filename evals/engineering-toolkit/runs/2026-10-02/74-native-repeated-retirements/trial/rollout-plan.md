# Billing-export revision 7 → 8: operator release plan

**Decision: HOLD.** This is local preparation, not authorization to change the cluster. Do not start the ordinary Deployment RollingUpdate or remove version-2 assets on the supplied evidence. Revision 7 has no proven safe retirement path for its 12–24-hour jobs, revision 8 has not demonstrated resumption of a format-2 checkpoint, and neither successor capacity nor recovery after a format-3 write has been rehearsed. Reconsider the hold only after the gates below are implemented and observed in a representative environment.

## Contract and present risk

- The unit of work is (tenant_id, export_id), across deliveries and revisions. Each accepted export needs one durable final result or a terminal business failure with its recorded cause. Controlled maintenance leaves it eligible to continue. Keep the business-failure limit at **three**, including for malformed input; do not raise it to mask rollout interruptions. [service-contract.md](service-contract.md)
- Readiness changes HTTP routing only. A receive can return after shutdown begins, and buffered deliveries can still enter a handler. The current poll loop has no admission gate. The three-retirement fixture drives healthy imp-884 through business failures 1, 2, 3 to the dead letter queue without a result. [worker.py](worker.py), [observations.json](observations.json)
- The platform has **one 90-second termination clock**, including the 15-second sleeping preStop. That leaves at most 75 seconds after SIGTERM, but the worker waits 85. Five seconds is a typical checkpoint latency, not a maximum. The PDB and RollingUpdate settings do not prove job handoff or capacity. [deployment.json](deployment.json)
- Current save_progress is not fenced with the effect. An old epoch-41 worker can resume after an epoch-42 worker commits cursor 600 and write cursor 500. A batch RPC timeout is ambiguous: the imp-885 receipt confirms rows and cursor 500 despite the timeout. A lost final ACK response is resolved by the stored final receipt. [service-contract.md](service-contract.md), [observations.json](observations.json)
- Revision 7 reads/writes format 2. Revision 8 reads formats 2/3 but defaults to writing 3. Its new-job smoke check does not exercise format-2 continuation. Revision 7 cannot resume unfinished format-3 imp-901. Three Ready revision-8 pods are not grounds to remove accepted-manifest-v2 or export-layout-v2. [deployment.json](deployment.json), [observations.json](observations.json)

## Gates before the first retirement

1. **Freeze the candidate and establish control.** Record worker image/configuration and queue/storage adapter identities, deployed revisions, controller state, termination hook, input/layout versions, and every accepted nonterminal job by key, checkpoint format/cursor, claim epoch and server lease expiry, operation and final receipts, business failures and maintenance deferrals. Measure the queue redelivery and successor claim/start delays against the proposed per-owner observation window below. Prove the controller can stage a revision-8 successor and pause before *each* revision-7 retirement. An ordinary unpaused RollingUpdate does not provide that control here.
2. **Provide a safe predecessor path.** The running revision-7 excerpt cannot be assumed to gain a drain handler. Before retiring any original revision-7 owner, use a **demonstrated** external admission/claim gate to prevent new revision-7 work; settle its in-flight receive and buffered/reserved deliveries; let active jobs reach confirmed final receipts or valid recorded terminal business failures with causes; and establish that no old writer can resume. A revision-7 repair is an alternative only after that repair itself is safely introduced and rehearsed. Do not force-kill or hand off an original revision-7 job while its unfenced save_progress can still write. If neither path exists, keep the rollout held; an export lasting beyond 90 seconds is not a reason to extend the platform deadline or spend a business retry. At **24 hours after closing admission**, reconcile every still-active revision-7 job and escalate any unfinished or unobservable job to the service owner; keep its owner and dependencies alive and stop the rollout rather than silently extending the retirement window.
3. **Qualify revision 8 and the storage path.** Implement and rehearse an atomic admission stop: set the poll stop and handler/claim gate before retirement, settle a receive returning after the gate, account for buffered/reserved/active deliveries, and reject further claims. For active work use stable batch operation IDs with epoch-checked commit_batch, so rows and cursor commit together; renew ownership until the handoff decision. On timeout, inspect the same operation ID and key before retrying. Confirm a compatible checkpoint receipt before maintenance_defer; that operation increments only maintenance_deferrals and releases the current epoch. Reserve business_fail for actual business errors. Require an epoch-checked finish receipt before completion ACK; on lost ACK response/redelivery, inspect and ACK the existing result. Prove a stale epoch can neither commit nor release a successor claim. An unconfirmed effect or receipt is unresolved, not a successful drain.
4. **Bound shutdown by the original platform deadline.** Replace the sleeping hook with a tested admission-closing path that completes promptly; make SIGTERM close the same gate idempotently. Measure from the platform termination start, not from the end of preStop. Stop new write/checkpoint attempts by **T+80 seconds**; complete in-process cleanup and exit by **T+85**, or earlier if the measured cleanup maximum requires it, leaving at least five seconds before the forced kill at T+90. If the hook or cleanup cannot meet those bounds, hold the rollout. If a checkpoint cannot be confirmed in time, stop new writes, leave the delivery unacked and the job visible, and record its key and stable operation ID for post-exit receipt reconciliation after server lease expiry under a new epoch. Do not call maintenance_defer without its compatible checkpoint receipt. Record remaining active/reserved work and cleanup of owned tasks, sockets and datastore sessions. This forced-deadline path is suitable only for fenced workers; original revision 7 must satisfy gate 2 instead.
5. **Prove continuation, capacity and format policy.** Rehearse revision 8 taking an actual revision-7 format-2 checkpoint and advancing the next 100-row batch using retained accepted-manifest-v2 and export-layout-v2. Show enough real queue and export capacity to continue accepted work as each owner retires; a Ready pod alone is insufficient. **Before any revision-8 format-3 progress, including staging or smoke work,** either pin every revision-8 writer to format 2 for the mixed-version interval with tested configuration/semantics, or demonstrably exclude every revision-7 worker from new, late and buffered claims on work that may carry format 3. Until one of those conditions is proved, keep staged revision-8 workers from writing. Before actually enabling format 3, also prove a revision-8-compatible forward recovery route and explicitly close revision-7-only rollback. Retain revision-8-capable capacity and v2/v3 dependencies for all unfinished jobs that need them. The current default is not a tested format policy.
6. **Set observation and ownership.** Expose per-key admission, receive-in-flight, buffered/reserved/active ownership, revision, epoch/lease, batch operation and final receipts, cursor/format, business-failure and maintenance-deferral counts, queue age, and successor claim/resume outcomes. Identify the named release operator who can freeze the controller, stop further retirements and retain dependencies; identify the service owner for escalation. These people are not supplied by the bundle, so assignment is a release gate. The worker lease owner and the human owner of post-rollout dependency retirement are distinct.

## Conditional, one-owner-at-a-time procedure

1. With the controller paused and baseline recorded, stage compatible revision-8 capacity without automatically deleting a revision-7 pod. Keep it from writing until the mixed-version format gate above is met; then verify the new pod is Ready **and** can claim and resume representative accepted work with expected receipts, progress and throughput. If staging itself causes retirement, stop: the required controller gate is absent.
2. For one revision-7 owner at a time (A, then B, then C only if those are still the actual owners), close its authoritative queue/claim admission and reconcile in-flight receives, buffered, reserved and active work. For the original revision-7 code, wait for every active job to have a durable final receipt or valid recorded terminal business failure with cause, and for no old writer to remain before termination or successor claim. For a separately qualified fenced owner, use the T+80 checkpoint/defer or lease-expiry reconciliation rule above. Record each logical job's key, initial/final cursor, batch/result receipts, old/new epochs and both counters; completion ACK follows the final receipt.
3. Observe the successor actually claim/resume the old owner's unfinished work after server lease expiry/redelivery when applicable. Set a **15-minute observation deadline after the later of old-process exit and server-confirmed lease expiry** for each owner; rehearse that queue redelivery and successor claim/start normally fit this window before GO. If any unfinished job has not resumed by that deadline, freeze further retirements, inspect its key/receipts/lease and escalate to the service owner while retaining compatible capacity and inputs. Confirm no cursor regression, duplicate rows, lost result, maintenance charge to business failures, or stranded delivery; confirm queue progress and adequate remaining capacity. Only then authorize the next retirement. The platform's nominal two available pods is a floor to verify, not proof of spare export capacity.
4. Keep the rollout controller paused between owners. After the last replacement, reconcile the full accepted-job inventory against durable final results, recorded terminal business failures with causes, and still-nonterminal jobs with compatible owners/inputs and an explicit next action. The latter remain operational obligations even if all revision-8 pods are Ready.

## Stop, recovery and dependency retirement

Freeze further retirements and format changes if admission remains open after the gate, a late receive/claim cannot be accounted for, an old writer survives handoff, successor capacity or format-2 continuation fails, a batch/result receipt is ambiguous, the cursor regresses or rows duplicate, maintenance increments business failures, the bounded shutdown or per-owner observation deadline is missed, or any accepted job lacks a visible continuation path. Keep required inputs/layouts and existing viable owners. Inspect the **same logical key and stable operation IDs** to reconcile possible commits before replaying, ACKing or releasing; use server-confirmed lease expiry and epoch fencing before another claim. Escalate an unresolved job to the service owner; never call it preserved solely because the process exited or a replacement is Ready.

Revision-7-only rollback is unsafe after any format-3 progress. Even while all checkpoints remain format 2, the current revision-7 shutdown/write path has the defects above; use revision 7 for recovery only after its own admission, fencing and retirement conditions are proven. For format-3 work, retain revision-8-compatible workers and recover forward with a tested compatible revision, unless a separately tested conversion is supplied. Do not delete version-2 inputs/layouts or disable old format readers on a pod-count trigger.

**Proposed post-rollout accountability:** the named billing-export release operator owns the unfinished-job and asset inventory after the last pod replacement; the service owner receives an escalation if any job lacks confirmed continuation, and a formal review **24 hours after the last pod replacement** for work still unfinished. Review daily thereafter until all jobs needing v2 assets have final receipts or a documented supported recovery route no longer needs those assets. Release accepted-manifest-v2 and export-layout-v2 only after that inventory and recovery window are checked and the assigned owner signs off. This is a review/escalation deadline, not a deadline for forcibly ending a 12–24-hour export. Without a named operator and this evidence, asset retirement remains blocked.

## Local evidence and rehearsal still required

The completed local command python3 observe.py projected only authored arithmetic: business counts [1,2,3] versus maintenance [0,0,0] in the retirement schedule; poison control also [1,2,3]; two buffered deliveries plus imp-889 returning after signal; 75 seconds after the current hook versus an 85-second wait; receipt/cursor 500 after checkpoint timeout; epoch 42 before the stale write; stored final receipt result-890; revision-7 reads format 2 and revision-8 defaults to format 3. This is **synthetic artifact inspection**, not execution of workers, adapters, signals or Kubernetes. The service contract and fixture explicitly say so.

Before a GO decision, run failure-oriented rehearsals against the actual candidate and queue/storage adapters: three successive retirements of one healthy long export without business-budget loss; a malformed export reaching the unchanged third business failure; SIGTERM with active, buffered and late-returning receive work; bounded preStop/SIGTERM and forced checkpoint delay through the original 90-second deadline; timeout after committed commit_batch with stable-ID reconciliation; paused old epoch versus successor commit/release; lost final ACK response; revision-8 continuation from format 2 with v2 inputs; format-3 recovery and attempted revision-7-only rollback; repeated controller pauses and one-by-one replacement with real successor capacity. Capture initial/final keys, effects/rows, cursors, receipts, epochs, both counters, lease/deadline layers, queue state and process/socket/session cleanup. Verify the harmful schedules fail on the old candidate and the corrected candidate finishes or reaches its specified recoverable state. Obtain independent review of the frozen image/configuration and procedure after those checks. No production or cluster observation is in this bundle.
