copies, with the unchanged runner and dependency files from
[runner-snapshot-07](../runner-snapshot-07/snapshot.json).

checks against the unchanged raw cases, prompts, rubrics and fixtures.

separately. It does not replace an earlier result or grant acceptance. Candidate
bodies remain bound to their original trials; no model, host run, grading, or
candidate rewrite occurs here.
