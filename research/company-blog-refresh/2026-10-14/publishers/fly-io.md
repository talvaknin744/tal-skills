## Useful additions and limits

Litestream 0.5 introduces LTX compaction and transaction identifiers for restore history. Its new executable cannot restore the older 0.3 WAL segment format even though configuration is compatible and old files remain intact. A deferred `recovery-validation` addition should bind restore artifacts to a compatible restore executable and format, keeping required older tooling through the supported rollback window. The discriminating case supplies a valid old backup and a new restore executable that accepts the configuration but cannot read its format. This research ran no restore or durability benchmark. [Complete Litestream release account](https://fly.io/blog/litestream-v050-is-here/)

The LiteFS consistency account uses a client's acknowledged write position to wait for a replica before serving its dependent read. Its cookie and HTTP method proxy design does not cover arbitrary websocket or cookie free traffic, and it promises read your writes rather than every stronger consistency contract. `concurrency-correctness/references/replica-reads.md` already owns this contract, so the article adds confirming evidence. Check current deployed LiteFS behavior before using historical platform details; the article's LiteFS Cloud offering was later retired. [Complete consistency article](https://www.fly.io/blog/tracking-consistency-with-litefs/), [retirement notice](https://www.fly.io/blog/litefs-cloud/)
