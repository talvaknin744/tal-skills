Research date: 14 October 2026. The retained scope is `/en/blog/{article}`, excluding category, product, tag, archive directories and other locales. The official sitemap and legacy feed expose 1563 routes, matching the retained baseline. The public archive index, robots request and three representative article requests return an HTTP 429 access checkpoint. The collector stopped host traffic with three attempted bodies blocked and 1560 bodies pending. This collection is incomplete.

Two selected primary article bodies were read through the web research tool, independently of the blocked direct HTTP crawl. That transport distinction remains explicit in [the receipt](hashicorp.json). Their availability through the research tool does not establish access to every direct crawl body or close the archive discovery gap. No challenge bypass or alternate locale collection was attempted.

The discovery helper initially normalized the publisher hostname to its www alias. The primary sitemap and verified baseline instead agree on `hashicorp.com` and the same 1563 article paths. Reconciliation restored those original identities and preserved the duplicate state snapshot, raw requests and every earlier receipt externally. The original custom probe did not freeze its imported collector candidate hash; the wrapper and observed HTTP evidence remain attributable, while common checkpoint candidate hashes are reported separately.

## Useful additions and limits

The ephemeral values account distinguishes `sensitive` output redaction from values omitted from plan and state artifacts. Ephemeral values and supported write only arguments avoid persistence; version attributes provide rotation intent because omitted values cannot be compared from state. Ephemeral resources participate in dependencies and can execute during plan and apply, with provider specific open, renew and close behavior. A generated credential needs an independently recoverable secret authority. [Complete ephemeral values account](https://www.hashicorp.com/en/blog/ephemeral-values-in-terraform)

This is a deferred extension to `infrastructure-change-safety`, rather than a new Terraform package. A discriminating case lets a secret manager credential update succeed and a dependent database credential update fail. Recovery must preserve or reconcile the intended credential and version, rather than silently generate a different password on resume. A nontrigger asks only for a resource address move. Verify installed Terraform and provider support, related version attributes, artifact exposure and consumer reload before implementation. The release article documents introduction in Terraform 1.11, not universal provider coverage. Current sensitive data documentation continues to require protection of state. This research executed no Terraform apply or secret rotation. [Complete release account](https://www.hashicorp.com/en/blog/terraform-1-11-ephemeral-values-managed-resources-write-only-arguments), [current sensitive data contract](https://developer.hashicorp.com/terraform/language/manage-sensitive-data)
