## Reading and recommendations

### agent-evaluation

Score task success against intent and constraints, with separate normal, degraded-tool and ambiguous-input cases. Inspect observable tool calls and side effects, expected schemas, tool budgets and failure categories. Report tokens, tool calls and latency per successful task alongside correctness, because final answers can conceal expensive or unsafe trajectories.

Proposed discriminating evaluation cases:

- Identical answers conceal a repeated external side effect.
- A degraded tool triggers unbounded retries.
- A fast answer omits required intent or constraints.
- A deterministic local unit test does not need a model evaluation harness.
