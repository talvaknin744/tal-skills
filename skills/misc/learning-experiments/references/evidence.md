# Evidence boundaries

The workflow is an original adaptation of coaching ideas; it is not a validated diagnostic instrument or universal optimization method.

- [Sung's habit-replacement procedure](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zKMZs6dLwPQ&t=1456s): use a concrete cue, replacement action and reflection. Adopt the practical experiment, not a claimed neural mechanism or speed multiplier.
- [Sung's confusion question](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nIABz0Z4IRA&t=156s): turn a vague gap into an answerable question. Confusion itself does not prove progress.
- [Sung's paper-thinking revision](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tGrMjRqQGVY&t=680s): revise grouping and relationships. Preserve mathematical precision and verify provisional claims.
- [Sung's contextual practice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RytJr22inm8&t=528s): reconnect component practice with intended use. Transfer must be checked.
- [Sung's effort qualification](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebgl_3MlXM8&t=108s): high load can come from irrelevant difficulty. The same video's categorical low-effort claims are not adopted.
- [Sung's process audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QeOfsMHc7E4&t=414s) motivates inspecting one real topic's path through study; [the livestream qualification](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mQtSv7ZbbIw&t=3255s) supports reducing overload rather than treating maximum strain as the objective.

Use the source to identify the speaker's advice, then distinguish the skill's own proposed application. Full caption reading does not audit audio, visuals or every cited study. Anecdotes about exceptional learners and neuroscience metaphors do not establish expected outcomes for this learner.
