# Session patterns

Use actual availability; these are design defaults, not scientifically optimal doses.

| Purpose in a 120-minute appointment | Minutes | Observable result |
|---|---:|---|
| Older material, source closed | 15 | Attempt with assistance recorded |
| Question-led preview or explanation | 15 | Relevant conditions and one resolved question |
| Worked/faded example if needed | 10 | Learner explains the decisive step |
| Fresh independent practice | 50 | Complete justified attempt |
| Feedback and repair | 15 | First consequential error repaired |
| Exit record | 5 | Next action and review target |
| Breaks | 10 | Included in the appointment |

For a five-minute session, use one consequential recall or application question, then record the next action. The shortened appointment does not complete the displaced work. Keep long problems and mock simulations as substantial sessions rather than breaking every task into microlearning.

For lifelong learning, choose a useful deliverable such as explaining a decision, building a small working example or performing a procedure. Distinguish an immediate project from building broader foundations. Study a manageable part, attempt the real task, get competent feedback, then test a varied context after a delay. Keep low-stakes lookup material searchable when independent memorization is not part of the goal.

Near an exam, preserve retrieval, fresh course problems and feedback while adjusting one small habit. Calculate the time displaced before adding technique videos, mapping systems or new apps. Research work and website engineering are not student study hours.
