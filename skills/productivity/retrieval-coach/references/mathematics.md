# Mathematics coaching

Check domain, hypotheses, quantifier order, parameter boundaries, calculations and proof completeness. A correct final value with invalid reasoning is not a fully justified solution. An unfamiliar valid method deserves mathematical checking, not rejection for differing from the template.

Choose the demand deliberately: stating a theorem; recognizing applicability without a theorem-name cue; executing a procedure; proving a claim; identifying a false converse; or working under an agreed time limit. Success at one demand does not certify the others. Compare generated questions with permitted reviewed course examples when available; otherwise mark exam-level alignment unverified.

Preserve reserved mock papers, their solutions, subparts and duplicate appearances until the learner explicitly confirms completion and releases them. Calendar passage alone is insufficient. If the requested source is protected, use a newly authored general concept question without deriving it from that paper. When archive coverage is unknown, say so.

Use a counterexample only after establishing that the proposed assertion is false. Keep definitions and theorem conditions exact when using a picture, map or analogy. Ask what a connection means: implication, equivalence, prerequisite, contrast or analogy. Test a questionable arrow before using it to justify a solution.

## Hebrew prompts

Choose one appropriate prompt; avoid turning a short session into a checklist.

- Attempt: **מה צריך להוכיח או לחשב? איזו שיטה מתאימה, ואילו תנאים מאפשרים להשתמש בה? כתוב את הניסיון שלך לפני שנבדוק.**
- Clarify a blockage: **מה בדיוק חסר כרגע — הגדרה, תנאי משפט, בחירת שיטה או צעד בחישוב?**
- Inspect a proof: **מהו המעבר הראשון שעדיין לא נימקת, ואיזה משפט יכול להצדיק אותו?**
- Compare: **מה דומה בין שני המקרים, מה שונה, ואיזה הבדל משנה את בחירת השיטה?**
- Calibration: **לפני המשוב, עד כמה אתה בטוח בתשובה, מ־0 עד 100?**
- Transfer: **איך תפתור מקרה חדש שבו התנאי הזה משתנה?**

If the user wants English explanations and Hebrew questions, retain that split. Adapt language when requested.
